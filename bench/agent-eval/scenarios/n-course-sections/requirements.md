# Requirements: Course sections

1. Record courses with a unique course code and a title.
2. A section of a course has a section number and a semester; the same section number can be reused in another semester or for another course, but no course has two sections with the same number in the same semester.
3. Each section belongs to exactly one course; a course may have no sections or any number of sections.
4. Record professors with a unique professor number and a name.
5. Each section is taught by exactly one professor; a professor may teach no sections or any number of sections.
6. Record students with a unique student number and a name.
7. A student may take no sections or any number of sections; a section may have no students or any number of students.
8. For every student taking a section, keep that student’s grade for that section.
9. A course may require no other courses or any number of other courses beforehand, and it may be required beforehand by no courses or any number of courses.
10. Record departments with a unique department code and a name.
11. Each department is chaired by exactly one professor; a professor may chair no department or at most one department.
