# Requirements: Supplies for projects

1. Record suppliers with a unique supplier number, a name and a city.
2. Record parts with a unique part number, a description and a colour.
3. Record projects with a unique project number, a name and a budget.
4. Record warehouses with a unique warehouse number and a location.
5. Each part is stored in exactly one warehouse; a warehouse may be empty or store any number of parts.
6. When a supplier delivers a particular part to a particular project, record the quantity for that supplier, part and project together.
7. Keep one total quantity for each such combination, rather than a list of individual shipments.
8. A supplier may make no deliveries or deliver many different parts to many projects; a part may have no deliveries or be delivered by many suppliers to many projects; a project may receive no deliveries or receive many parts from many suppliers.
9. Knowing who supplies which part and which project uses which part does not tell which supplier delivered that part to that project.
