# Requirements: Veterinary clinic

1. For a veterinary clinic, record each owner with a unique owner number and a name made up of a first name and a last name.
2. Keep several phone numbers for an owner; the owner may provide none or any number of them.
3. A pet is known by its name only within its owner’s records: names may repeat for different owners, but an owner never has two pets with the same name.
4. Each pet has exactly one owner; an owner may have no pets or any number of pets.
5. For each pet record its birth date and show its age calculated from that date, without storing a separately entered age.
6. Record each vet with a unique vet number and a name.
7. A vet may treat no pets or any number of pets, and a pet may be treated by no vets or any number of vets.
8. For every vet and pet who have met for treatment, keep the date of their last treatment together; dates can differ for different vets treating the same pet.
9. A senior vet may mentor no junior vets or any number of junior vets; each junior vet may have no mentor or at most one senior mentor.
10. Senior and junior describe the two duties in mentoring, and all these people are recorded among the same vets.
